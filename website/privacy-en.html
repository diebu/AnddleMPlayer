AnddleMPlayer

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

AnddleMPlayer ("the App") is a local-file music player for iPhone. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect, upload, or transmit personal information. It contains no advertising, user tracking, behavioral analytics, crash-reporting, or third-party marketing SDKs, and it does not use an advertising identifier to track you.

2. Local Files and Playback Data

  • You import audio files only through the iOS system file picker.
  • File names, audio metadata, and artwork are read and displayed only on your device.
  • Playlists, playback progress, playback speed, sleep timers, and recent import locations are stored locally on your device.
  • Caches and imported copies created by the App remain inside its local application sandbox.

3. System Permissions

The App accesses only files you explicitly select through iOS system interfaces. It does not access files you have not selected and does not bypass iOS privacy or permission controls. Background audio and media controls use standard iOS audio capabilities.

4. Sharing and Sale of Data

The App does not sell, rent, share, or disclose personal information to third parties. Because the App does not collect personal data, it has no transfer process for that purpose.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, social login, or cloud-storage services. Apple services such as the App Store, iOS, and iCloud backup are governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policy and are outside the developer's control.

6. Retention and Deletion

Imported files, caches, and settings remain on your device. You can delete imported files and folders in the App. Uninstalling the App deletes data in its application sandbox. Any device backups you create are managed by you or Apple's backup service.

7. Data Security

The App relies on the iOS application sandbox and device security features to protect local data. Music and playback information are not uploaded to the developer's servers.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from any user, including children. If you believe there is a related concern, please contact us using the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

If the App's data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated. Material changes may also be noted in the App's release notes.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy or the App's data practices, contact: diebu@126.com
